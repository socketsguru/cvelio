Senior DevOps Engineer - Northwind Freight (Remote, Americas)

Northwind Freight moves 40,000 shipments a day for retailers across North America. Our platform team keeps 60 microservices running for 90 product engineers.

What you will do
- Own our AWS infrastructure as code with Terraform, including EKS clusters, networking and IAM.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, ArgoCD) so teams can deploy many times a day with confidence.
- Run Kubernetes in production: upgrades, autoscaling, cost control.
- Define SLOs with product teams and build observability with Prometheus, Grafana and Datadog.
- Lead incident response, write blameless postmortems and take part in the on-call rotation.
- Harden the platform: secrets management, least-privilege IAM, SOC 2 controls.

What we are looking for
- 5+ years in DevOps, SRE or platform engineering roles.
- Deep, hands-on Terraform and AWS experience.
- Production Kubernetes experience and solid Linux and networking fundamentals.
- Scripting in Python, Go or Bash.
- Experience reducing deployment time or infrastructure cost, with numbers to show for it.

Nice to have
- GitOps with ArgoCD or Flux, Helm charts.
- Experience building an internal developer platform (Backstage).
- AWS certifications.
