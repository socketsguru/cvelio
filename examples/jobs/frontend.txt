Senior Frontend Engineer - Lumen Health (Hybrid, Austin TX)

Lumen Health builds the patient portal used by 300 clinics. Patients book visits, message their care team and pay bills in our web app. We are a product team of 25.

The role
- Build accessible, responsive interfaces in React and TypeScript with Next.js.
- Own features end to end with designers and product managers, from Figma to production.
- Grow our design system and component library in Storybook.
- Improve performance: Core Web Vitals, bundle size, rendering strategy.
- Write unit and end-to-end tests with Jest, React Testing Library and Playwright or Cypress.
- Build real-time features such as messaging and notifications.

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional frontend development.
- Expert React and TypeScript; strong HTML, CSS and browser fundamentals.
- Experience meeting WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
- A track record of measurable performance or conversion improvements.
- Experience consuming REST or GraphQL APIs and managing client state.

Bonus
- Healthcare or other regulated-industry experience (HIPAA).
- Experience mentoring other engineers.
- Tailwind CSS.
