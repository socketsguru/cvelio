Senior Backend Engineer - Tessera Payments (Remote, LATAM)

Tessera processes card and bank payments for 8,000 merchants. Our backend handles 2,000 requests per second and money cannot go missing.

What you will do
- Design and build event-driven microservices in Go.
- Model data in PostgreSQL and DynamoDB, and use Redis for caching and rate limiting.
- Design clear REST and gRPC APIs for internal teams and merchants.
- Build idempotent, auditable payment flows and reconciliation processes.
- Own your services in production on AWS and Kubernetes: monitoring, on-call, performance tuning.
- Keep us compliant: PCI DSS, secure coding, OWASP Top 10.

What we are looking for
- 6+ years of backend experience, at least 3 with Go.
- Experience with messaging systems such as Kafka or SQS.
- Strong SQL and NoSQL data modelling.
- Fintech, banking or payments experience.
- Experience with distributed systems concerns: consistency, retries, idempotency.

Nice to have
- Python.
- Infrastructure as code with Terraform.
- Experience passing security audits or penetration tests.
