Data Analyst - Brightcart (Remote)

Brightcart is an online marketplace for independent grocers. The analytics team of five answers the questions that shape pricing, delivery and growth.

What you will do
- Write SQL against our Snowflake warehouse to answer questions from operations, finance and marketing.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Looker or Tableau that business teams use every day.
- Model data with dbt and keep metric definitions consistent.
- Design and analyse A/B tests and explain the results to people who are not statisticians.
- Automate recurring reports and data quality checks with Python.
- Turn findings into clear recommendations for stakeholders.

What you bring
- 3+ years in a data analyst or analytics engineering role.
- Advanced SQL: window functions, CTEs, query optimisation.
- Python for analysis (pandas) and comfort with statistics: hypothesis testing, regression.
- Experience with a BI tool and with data modelling.
- Clear writing and stakeholder communication.

Nice to have
- Airflow or another orchestrator.
- Experience in e-commerce, logistics or payments.
- Familiarity with event tracking and Google Analytics.
